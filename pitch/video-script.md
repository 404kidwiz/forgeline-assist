# ForgeLine Assist — 90-second investor demo video script

Companion to [storyboard.md](storyboard.md). Grounded in [../PROJECT_SUMMARY.md](../PROJECT_SUMMARY.md), [../tasks/plan.md](../tasks/plan.md) and [../DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) section 1. Planning artifact — no footage, voice track or edited video exists yet.

## Logline

When a mislabeled batch puts safety, maintenance and quality on the clock, ForgeLine Assist investigates across approved documentation, drafts the follow-up work, and asks a real supervisor to approve the one action that actually leaves the building.

## Full voiceover script

**0:00–0:08 — Hook**
"PK-04 just printed the wrong label on batch B-204. Maintenance, quality, and safety all need answers — right now."

**0:08–0:18 — Product intro**
"This is ForgeLine Assist: one agentic workspace for plant supervisors, built around Ask, Procedures, Manual, and Shift Desk. Maya just asks, in her own words."

**0:18–0:30 — Agentic search**
"Watch it think out loud. The agent searches maintenance, quality, and safety documentation in parallel, live in the activity feed — every step visible, nothing hidden."

**0:30–0:44 — Grounded answer**
"The answer comes back grounded in the exact approved source — document, revision, section. Maya opens the citation herself and reads the real page before she trusts a word of it."

**0:44–0:54 — Tasks + handover**
"ForgeLine Assist creates the follow-up tasks right there, and drafts the shift handover — so the next supervisor isn't starting from zero."

**0:54–1:06 — Approval**
"When it's time to touch a real system, it drafts a GitHub issue — but never sends it. Maya reviews the exact text and gives the one approval that fires it."

**1:06–1:18 — Trust boundary + monitoring**
"That approval step, and a hard line it won't cross in an emergency, are the design, not an afterthought. When a source changes, ForgeLine Assist notices — and alerts her, in-app and by email."

**1:18–1:30 — Close**
"ForgeLine Manufacturing is a fictional plant — built to prove a real workflow. ForgeLine Assist: ask, investigate, approve. Let's bring it to your floor."

## Tone notes per beat

| Beat | Pace | Energy |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Slightly quicker, short sentences | Urgent but controlled — stakes, not panic |
| 2. Product intro | Settling to a steady, unhurried pace | Warm, confident — establish credibility calmly |
| 3. Agentic search | Crisp, observational | Let the on-screen activity carry the energy; narrate, don't hype |
| 4. Grounded answer | Steady, deliberate | Reassuring precision — no triumphant sting, just proof |
| 5. Tasks + handover | Brisk, efficient | Practical relief — work getting organized, not fanfare |
| 6. Approval | Slower on "never sends it" and "one approval" | The trust beat — let it breathe, no rush |
| 7. Trust boundary + monitoring | Firm, even | State the design plainly and without defensiveness |
| 8. Close | Slower, warm | Quietly confident close; matches the editorial welcome typography, not a hard sell |
